What happens next

  1. Your input is checked in the browser. Nothing is sent until every field is valid.
  2. The API filters out materials that are unsafe for your product.
  3. The models predict cost and CO₂ for the rest and rank them.
  4. The ranked materials appear below the form; "View charts & export" opens the full analysis.
