How it works

1

Describe your product

Category, weight, fragility and how it ships.

2

Filter unsafe options

Materials must suit the category, carry the weight, protect fragile items, resist moisture at sea and be at least 70% recyclable.

3

Predict cost and CO₂

A Random Forest predicts cost per unit; XGBoost predicts CO₂ per kg for every remaining material.

4

Rank and compare

A weighted score (balanced, sustainability-first or cost-first) orders the materials; you see the top 5.
